How it works

Three steps to the mic

  1. 01

    Pick a room and hours

    Log in or sign up, then choose a room, a date, and the hours you want. Booked hours can't be selected.

  2. 02

    Pay within 15 minutes

    If you don't pay in time, the booking expires and the slot opens up for other guests.

  3. 03

    Show up and sing

    Once your payment goes through, the status changes to Confirmed. Just arrive at your booked time.
